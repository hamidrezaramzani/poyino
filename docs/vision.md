# Poyino Vision

Poyino is an AI-powered recruitment platform built to simplify and modernize the hiring process.

Instead of collecting resumes from multiple platforms and manually reviewing hundreds of applications, organizations publish a single job posting through Poyino and receive all candidates in one intelligent workspace.

Poyino automatically extracts information from resumes, analyzes candidates using artificial intelligence, ranks applicants based on job requirements, and helps hiring teams make faster and better hiring decisions.

Our goal is not to replace recruiters.

Our goal is to eliminate repetitive work so recruiters can focus on evaluating people instead of reading documents.

---

# The Problem

Hiring today is fragmented.

Companies publish job openings across multiple platforms such as LinkedIn, job boards, Telegram channels, company websites, and social media.

Applications arrive from different sources in different formats.

Recruiters spend hours:

- Collecting resumes
- Organizing files
- Reading PDFs
- Comparing candidates
- Extracting information manually
- Ranking applicants

Most of this work is repetitive and does not create value.

As hiring volume grows, the process becomes slower, more expensive, and more error-prone.

---

# Our Solution

Poyino provides one intelligent recruitment workflow.

Organizations create a job posting once.

A public application link is generated.

Candidates submit their resumes through that single link.

Poyino automatically:

- Parses resumes
- Extracts candidate information
- Detects skills and experience
- Generates AI summaries
- Calculates candidate-job matching
- Ranks applicants
- Organizes the hiring pipeline

Everything is managed inside one platform.

# Product Principles

Every feature inside Poyino should follow these principles.

## Simplicity

The product should feel easy to use.

Complex workflows should become simple.

---

## Intelligence

Artificial intelligence should reduce manual work, not create more.

---

## Speed

Recruiters should reach hiring decisions significantly faster than traditional workflows.

---

## Trust

AI recommendations should support human decisions, not replace them.

Users must always remain in control.

---

## Scalability

Every feature should be designed with future growth in mind.

The platform should evolve from a recruitment tool into a complete hiring ecosystem.
