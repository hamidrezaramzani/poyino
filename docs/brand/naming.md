# Origin

The name **Poyino** is inspired by the Persian root **"Poy" (پوی)**.

"Poy" represents movement, search, exploration, discovery, and the continuous pursuit of the right destination.

This reflects the core mission of Poyino:

Helping organizations discover the best talent through intelligent technology.

---

# Why Poyino?

Traditional hiring is mostly manual.

Recruiters search through hundreds of resumes, compare candidates, and spend significant time making hiring decisions.

Poyino exists to transform this process into an intelligent, AI-assisted experience.

The name symbolizes the continuous search for the right person.

# Naming Goals

The name was selected based on the following principles:

- Single-word
- Short
- Memorable
- Easy pronunciation
- Modern sound
- Brandable
- Suitable for logo design
- Suitable for mobile applications
- Suitable for global expansion

---

# Brand Positioning

Poyino is not just another Applicant Tracking System.

It is an intelligent hiring platform built around discovery.

The name reflects the idea that every organization is searching for the right talent, and every candidate is searching for the right opportunity.

Poyino connects both sides through intelligence.

---

# Future Compatibility

The name is intentionally independent of terms such as:

- Recruitment
- Hiring
- Resume
- Job
- HR

This allows the brand to expand into future products, including:

- Talent Management
- Internal Mobility
- AI Recruiting Assistant
- HR Analytics
- Career Platform
- Workforce Intelligence

without requiring a rebrand.
