# Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-09

---

# Brand Overview

Poyino is an AI-powered recruitment platform that helps organizations discover, evaluate, and hire the best talent through intelligent technology.

Our brand represents trust, intelligence, simplicity, and continuous discovery.

---

# Brand Mission

Help organizations make better hiring decisions through intelligent software.

---

# Brand Vision

Become the intelligent hiring platform organizations rely on to discover exceptional talent.

---

# Brand Personality

Poyino should always feel:

- Intelligent
- Trustworthy
- Professional
- Modern
- Minimal
- Human-centered
- Calm
- Confident

---

# Brand Values

## Simplicity

Complex hiring processes should become simple.

---

## Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence should assist people, not replace them.

---

## Trust

Every interaction should build confidence.

---

## Precision

Every recommendation should improve hiring quality.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Poyino evolves through learning, iteration, and customer feedback.

---

# Brand Voice

Our communication should be:

- Clear
- Direct
- Friendly
- Professional
- Helpful
- Honest

Avoid:

- Marketing buzzwords
- Overpromising
- Technical jargon when unnecessary
- Complex language

---

# Visual Identity

The visual language should communicate:

- Clarity
- Structure
- Confidence
- Precision

Design should always be:

- Clean
- Spacious
- Consistent
- Minimal

Avoid visual clutter.

---

# Logo Principles

The logo should be:

- Simple
- Memorable
- Scalable
- Flat
- Geometric
- Recognizable at small sizes

Avoid:

- Gradients
- 3D effects
- Heavy shadows
- Complex illustrations
- Trend-based effects

---

# Color Principles

Blue is the primary brand color because it represents:

- Trust
- Technology
- Stability

Accent colors should only emphasize important actions and information.

---

# Product Experience

Every screen should answer three questions immediately:

- Where am I?
- What can I do here?
- What should I do next?

If the user has to think, the interface should be simplified.

---

# Design Philosophy

Less, but better.

Every component should have a purpose.

Every interaction should reduce friction.

Every feature should solve a real problem.

---

# Brand Promise

Poyino helps organizations discover the right talent with confidence.

We build software that saves time, reduces manual work, and supports better hiring decisions.

---

# Long-Term Direction

Poyino is not just an Applicant Tracking System.

It is evolving into a complete AI-powered hiring platform.

Every product decision should support this long-term vision.
